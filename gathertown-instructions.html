STOC 2025 Online Poster Session

Gather.town: Poster Session Guide for Participants

1. How to Join

  • Click the Gather.Town link for the session.
  • You can choose your avatar for the arena.
  • Allow camera and/or microphone access so you can talk to the presenters.

2. Moving Around

  • Use your arrow keys or WASD keys to walk your avatar.
  • Approach posters and people — your video/audio connects automatically.

3. Visiting Posters and Asking Questions

  • Walk up to a poster — it will appear larger, or press ‘X’ to interact if prompted.
  • You can talk with the presenter naturally. Others nearby will hear too.

4. Other information

  • Mini-map: We will have multiple mini-maps of the posters in the main corridor and the individual rooms.
  • Participants List: See who’s online (bottom right) and where they are by clicking on their name.
  • Chat: Click the chat icon (bottom right) to message people individually or everyone at once.
Enjoy exploring the poster session!
Questions? Ask the hosts, or visit FAQs, or gather.town/help.
