STOC 2025 Online Poster Session

Call for Posters

June 23, 2025, 18:00 - 19:00 CEST (GMT +2)
Gather.Town and Prague, Czech Republic

Deadline for electronically submitting posters: June 13, 2025

The 57th ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 2025) will feature a online poster session, to engage both in-person attendees at STOC 2025 and community members who are unable to attend the conference physically. The goal of this session is to facilitate meaningful interactions between in-person, as well as online participants, while allowing more opportunities for authors, including those unable to attend in-person, to showcase their work to the broader TCS community. Authors who are not attending STOC will be especially encouraged to present; we believe this will encourage both the online and in-person attendees by providing opportunities for conversations that would otherwise not be available at the conference. Moreover, each poster is intended to serve as an accompaniment to the video, as well as the live talk component of its corresponding paper.

The online poster session will be held on Monday, June 23, 2025 from 18:00 to 19:00, CEST (Central European Summer Time, GMT +2) on Gather.Town. We welcome poster presentations for any papers that have been accepted at STOC 2025 and FOCS 2024.

Submission Instructions: Anyone wishing to present a poster, should submit it by Friday, June 13, 2025 at the Poster Submission Form. We kindly request that all the posters be submitted by this deadline. Any submissions received after the deadline will be considered at the discretion of the organisers.

The posters are intended to spark a longer discussion about the paper itself. A single page, static PDF, preferably in the 16*9 aspect ratio is recommended. There is no strict format and the presenter may choose whatever format that best showcases the paper - a full poster, or a few key slides from the conference talk, or even a single slide that encapsulates the work, and can lead to further conversations.

Format of the Poster Session: This event will be an analogue of an in-person poster session. Each presenter will have their own spot in a virtual "room" on Gather.Town. Attendees can freely walk around this room to browse any posters they like, and interact with the presenters.

We will announce the poster list and the arena layout on Gather.Town, closer to the day of the event. This will also be followed with instructions to use the Gather.Town platform, for those new to it.

For any further questions please reach out to: Ian Mertz, Ninad Rajgopal, or Hanlin Ren.
