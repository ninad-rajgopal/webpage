STOC 2025 Online Poster Session

Call for Posters

June 23, 2025, 18:00 - 19:00 CEST (GMT +2)
Gather.Town and Prague, Czech Republic

Poster registration deadline: May 26, 2025

Deadline for electronically submitting posters: June 10, 2025

The 57th ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 2025) will feature a online poster session, to engage both in-person attendees at STOC 2025 and community members who are unable to attend the conference physically. The goal of this session is to facilitate meaningful interactions between in-person, as well as online participants, while allowing more opportunities for authors, including those unable to attend in-person, to showcase their work to the broader TCS community. Authors who are not attending STOC will be especially encouraged to present; we believe this will encourage both the online and in-person attendees by providing opportunities for conversations that would otherwise not be available at the conference.

The online poster session will be held on Monday, June 23, 2025 from 18:00 to 19:00, CEST (Central European Summer Time, GMT +2) on Gather.Town. We welcome poster presentations for any papers that have been accepted at STOC 2025 and FOCS 2024. Moreover, posters pertaining to papers accepted at STOC 2025 will serve as an accompaniment to the video, and the live talk component.

Submission Instructions: Anyone wishing to present a poster should register their commitment by Monday, May 26, 2025 at the Poster Registration Form. Following this, emails will be sent to the presenters with instructions to submit their posters. The deadline for sending the electronic versions of the posters, in PDF, will be on Tuesday, June 10, 2025. Registrations or submissions received after the deadlines may be considered at the discretion of the organisers.

We will announce the list of posters and the layout in the arena on Gather.Town closer to the day of the event. This will also be followed with instructions to use the Gather.Town platform, for those new to it.

For any further questions please reach out to: Ian Mertz, Ninad Rajgopal, or Hanlin Ren.
